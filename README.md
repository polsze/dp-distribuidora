
# DP Distribuidora — Corporate Website

Corporate website developed for **DP Distribuidora SRL**, a company specializing in heavy machinery, road construction equipment, and spare parts distribution.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://www.dpdistribuidora.com.ar/

## 📌 About the Project

The website was designed to strengthen the company's digital presence, showcase its products and services, and provide potential customers with an accessible way to explore its catalog and contact the sales team.

Based in Posadas, Misiones, Argentina, DP Distribuidora serves construction, mining, industrial, agricultural, and road machinery sectors, with nationwide distribution.

## 🚀 Main Features

- Responsive design optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- Corporate landing page with product and service information.
- Heavy machinery showcase featuring LGMG and BAMAC equipment.
- Spare parts catalog organized by product categories.
- Brand and product presentation.
- WhatsApp integration for direct customer inquiries.
- Contact information and nationwide distribution coverage.
- SEO-oriented content and website structure.

## 🏗️ Products & Solutions

- Heavy machinery and road construction equipment.
- Engine and transmission components.
- Hydraulic systems and undercarriage parts.
- Electrical components.
- Cutting tools and wear parts.
- Donaldson filters and filtration solutions.
